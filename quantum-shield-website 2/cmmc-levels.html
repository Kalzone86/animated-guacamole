CMMC Guide

CMMC 2.0 levels explained: Level 1 vs. Level 2 vs. Level 3

Which level you need, what each one requires, and how it's assessed.

Quick answer: If you only handle Federal Contract Information (FCI), you need Level 1. If you handle Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), you need Level 2, either by self-assessment or by a C3PAO certification assessment, depending on your contract. Level 3 applies to a small number of programs with the most sensitive CUI.

Start with the data you handle

Your CMMC level is driven by the type of information you process, store or transmit for the Department of Defense (DoD), and by the level written into your solicitation or contract.

  • Federal Contract Information (FCI) is information provided by or generated for the government under a contract that isn't intended for public release. Almost every DoD contractor handles FCI.
  • Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) is information the government requires to be safeguarded, such as controlled technical information, drawings and specifications. CUI is what moves you to Level 2 or above.

CMMC Level 1 (Foundational)

  • Who: contractors that handle FCI but not CUI.
  • Requirements: the 15 basic safeguarding requirements from FAR 52.204-21.
  • Assessment: an annual self-assessment, with results entered in the Supplier Performance Risk System (SPRS).
  • Affirmation: a senior company official affirms compliance every year.
  • POA&Ms: not allowed. All 15 requirements must be met.

CMMC Level 2 (Advanced)

  • Who: contractors that handle CUI.
  • Requirements: the 110 security requirements in NIST SP 800-171 Revision 2.
  • Assessment: either a self-assessment or a certification assessment by an authorized Third-Party Assessment Organization (C3PAO), every three years. Your contract specifies which.
  • Affirmation: annual affirmation by a senior official.
  • POA&Ms: allowed only in limited form. You can reach Conditional status with a minimum score of 88 out of 110, only certain requirements may be left open, and every open item must be closed within 180 days.

CMMC Level 3 (Expert)

  • Who: contractors supporting DoD's highest-priority programs with the most sensitive CUI.
  • Prerequisite: a Final Level 2 certification from a C3PAO for the same scope.
  • Requirements: 24 additional requirements selected from NIST SP 800-172.
  • Assessment: conducted by the DCMA Defense Industrial Base Cybersecurity Assessment Center (DIBCAC), every three years, with annual affirmations.

How do I know which level my contract requires?

The required CMMC level appears in the solicitation and the resulting contract through DFARS 252.204-7021. If you're a subcontractor, your prime should flow the requirement down to you. When in doubt, ask your contracting officer or prime which level, and which type of Level 2 assessment, applies.

Where to start

  1. Define your scope. Identify where FCI and CUI live and which people, systems and facilities touch them.
  2. Run a gap assessment against the requirements for your level.
  3. Document your System Security Plan (SSP) and a plan to close gaps. Learn what goes in an SSP.
  4. Remediate, then validate before your self-assessment or C3PAO assessment.
