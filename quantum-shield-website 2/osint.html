The Threat Landscape

Executives are the target. Your public profile is the weapon.

Spear phishing campaigns targeting senior leadership are not random — they are researched, personalized, and built from information that is already publicly available. Before an adversary sends a single email, they have mapped your digital footprint, identified your relationships, and crafted a message designed specifically for you.

◈

Authority & Access

Executives hold signing authority, wire transfer approval, and access to sensitive systems. A successful spear phish against leadership can compromise an entire organization in a single click.

◉

Public Footprint

LinkedIn profiles, conference bios, press releases, public records, and social media collectively build a detailed picture of your life, schedule, relationships, and habits — all without a single intrusion.

◆

Social Engineering Risk

The more publicly visible you are, the more material an attacker has. Information you consider harmless — a travel post, a board affiliation, a family member's name — becomes a tool for manipulation.

Assessment Scope

Every publicly accessible layer of your digital identity.

Using only passive, non-intrusive techniques and publicly available sources, we build the same profile an adversary would construct before targeting you.

01

Social Media Exposure

Public-facing profiles across LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram, and other platforms. We identify what personal, professional, and location data is visible and how it can be leveraged.

02

Data Broker Records

People-search sites and data aggregators publish home addresses, phone numbers, relatives, and associated individuals. We document what these sources reveal about you.

03

Leaked Credentials

Email addresses and usernames associated with you are checked against known breach databases. Compromised credentials are a primary entry point for account takeover and targeted attacks.

04

Public Records

Property ownership, business filings, court records, voter registration, and other government databases that expose personal details, assets, and affiliations.

05

News & Media Coverage

Press releases, conference appearances, interviews, and articles that reveal your business decisions, travel patterns, organizational relationships, and public positions.

06

Corporate Affiliations

Board memberships, advisory roles, subsidiary connections, and organizational charts that expose your professional network and decision-making authority.

07

Personal Connections

Publicly visible family members, associates, and personal relationships that adversaries can exploit to build trust, context, or leverage in a targeted attack.

What You Receive

A complete written security profile — yours to keep.

Every assessment concludes with a detailed, confidential written report. This is not a generic checklist. It is a personalized analysis of your specific public exposure, the attack vectors it creates, and a clear path to reducing your risk.

01

Attack Surface Map

A full inventory of your publicly exposed data points, ranked by risk severity and exploitability.

02

Attack Vector Analysis

Specific scenarios showing how an adversary could weaponize your public information in a spear phishing or social engineering campaign.

03

Remediation Guidance

Prioritized, actionable steps you can take to reduce your exposure — including how to address data broker records and tighten your public profile.

04

Risk Acceptance Guidance

Not all exposure can be eliminated. We identify what residual risk you will need to accept and what that means for your day-to-day security posture.

05

Awareness & Training Recommendations

Targeted guidance on recognizing spear phishing attempts, verifying unusual requests, and building personal security habits that match your risk profile.

Scope of Service

Assessment and guidance — not data removal.

This is a passive intelligence assessment. We will not remove your data from third-party sites or platforms on your behalf — that falls outside the scope of this service. What we provide is a precise picture of what exists, where it lives, and a prioritized roadmap for how you can address it. You leave with the knowledge and the plan.
