Services & Engagement Options

Advisory services scoped to your CMMC 2.0 journey.

A structured evaluation of your current cybersecurity posture against CMMC 2.0 Level 1 or Level 2 requirements. You'll finish with a clear picture of where you stand against all 110 NIST SP 800-171 controls and a prioritized remediation roadmap.

Important Note

What an RP can and cannot do

Registered Practitioners are authorized by the Cyber-AB to provide non-certified advisory services. RPs do not conduct official CMMC assessments — that work is reserved for Certified Third-Party Assessment Organizations (C3PAOs). Our role is to prepare you for that assessment so you walk in ready to succeed.
